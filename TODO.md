- [ ] **Structural: loan identity collides when two loans share the same customer/location/end date** — `_loanKey()` (used almost everywhere to look up "which loan") is built purely from customer name + location + end date, with no BatchID in it. Two genuinely different loans (different BatchIDs) with identical details — e.g. an accidental duplicate booking — generate the *same* key, so actions like "Add items" can silently resolve to the wrong one. Root-caused a real incident this way (Conference Magic - QT Queenstown Hotel, 2026-09-15): an end-user's accidental duplicate booking, once returned and re-added to the correct loan, kept its own BatchID instead of merging, because the two loans were indistinguishable by key at the moment the action ran.
